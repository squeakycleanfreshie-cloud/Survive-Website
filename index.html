WHAT IS THIS EVENT?

Survive is a program where you make survival and offline themed tools/devices, and win camping and outdoors prizes. This event will potentially lead to a in-person outdoors and adventure hackathon.

Ship, Earn, Survive

FAQ

What is a YSWS? ▼
"You Ship We Ship" is a Hack Club program: you build something real and ship it, we send you something awesome in return.
Is this legit? ▼
100%. This is an official Hack Club program run by real teens who love building things.
Who can participate? ▼
Any teen aged 13-18. No experience needed. There will be tutorials
What can I make? ▼
Anything outdoors or offline themed. This could be a tool, a device, an app, a game.
When will it start? ▼
Very soon. RSVP now so you don't miss the start.
Who is running this? ▼
This was made entirely for teens, by teens.
What are the prizes? ▼
Real camping and outdoor gear — plus the chance to earn a spot at an in-person adventure hackathon.
If there is a hackathon, where will it be? ▼
We are currently deciding if there will be one, and where it will be located.

HOW IT WORKS

01 CREATE

02 SHIP

Submit your project
to the Survive team, for reviewing

03 REVIEW

The Survive team
checks your project for mistakes, and authenticity

04 EARN PRIZES

Hours get converted to wood, spend wood in the shop
on outdoors gear and event tickets
