FAQ

What is a YSWS? ▼
"You Ship We Ship" — a Hack Club program with a simple deal: you build something real and ship it, we send you something awesome in return.
Is this legit? ▼
100%. This is an official Hack Club program run by real teens who love building things.
Who can participate? ▼
Any teen aged 13-18. No experience needed — just the drive to build.
What can I make? ▼
Anything survival or offline themed. A tool, a device, an app — if it solves a real problem and works without the internet, you're in.
When will it start? ▼
Very soon. RSVP now so you don't miss the drop.
Who is running this? ▼
A crew of teens at Hack Club who wanted to build something wilder than your average hackathon.
What are the prizes? ▼
Real camping and outdoor gear — plus the chance to earn a spot at an in-person adventure hackathon in the wild.
If there is a hackathon, where will it be? ▼
We are currently deciding if there will be one, and where it will be located.
